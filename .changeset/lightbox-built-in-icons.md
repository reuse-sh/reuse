Every built-in control draws its own SVG icon in `currentColor` instead of a text glyph in the page font: close, previous, next, zoom in and out, fullscreen enter and exit, download, and slideshow play and pause. Zoom, slideshow and fullscreen switch their icon with their state. The `icons` option still replaces any of them by key.
