The `icons` option and a slot's `icon(name)` gain `zoomOut`, `fullscreenExit` and `slideshowPause`. The zoom, fullscreen and slideshow buttons show them while zoomed, in full screen and playing. Without one, a button keeps the icon you gave for its first state, or switches to the built-in second icon when you gave none.
