A zoomed photo now pans to the window's edges, also under the aside panel, the thumbnail strip and the room a page keeps for its own footer with the new `--re-lightbox-bottom-room` property, and only the close and zoom buttons come back while it is zoomed. A footer slot keeps its own zoom control by giving it the `re-lightbox-zoom` class. Dragging a zoomed photo shows the grabbing cursor instead of the default arrow.
