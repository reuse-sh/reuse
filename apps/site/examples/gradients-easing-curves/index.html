• ease-in-out-sine Quiet at both ends and quickest in the middle. The default, and the right one for most fades.

  • ease-in Stays light for longest and darkens near the end. Good for a scrim that should leave most of the image alone.

  • ease-out Darkens early and settles. Good when the solid end should reach further into the gradient.

  • linear One straight ramp with two stops. Its start and end show as lines on a large surface.
