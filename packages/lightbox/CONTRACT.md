# Shared lightbox interface

This contract is shared by everyone who builds on the lightbox: the carousel, the site's control styles and the products that adopt it. Keep these names and shapes stable. Additional options may be added only for an actual required behavior. Browser imports are safe before a DOM exists.

createLightbox<Item extends LightboxItem = LightboxItem>(options?: LightboxOptions<Item>): Lightbox<Item>. Item types open(), setItems(), append(), state.item, renderContent's item and the slots' lightbox, so an item type of your own, with fields such as an id, comes back as that type.

LightboxOptions:
- container?: HTMLElement, where the dialog is appended, default the body that the document of the opened photo's origin has at each open(), else the page's document; a gallery in a same-origin frame, bound from the parent page, opens in the frame without a container
- imageClick?: "zoom" | "close" | "none", default zoom; what a mouse click or a finger tap on the photo does. A tap waits out the 300ms double-tap window and then shows or hides the controls; "close" closes at once and turns off the double tap. A click or tap beside the photo closes
- doubleTapZoom?: boolean, default true, two taps within 300ms and 30px zoom
- maxZoom?: number, the most a photo zooms as a multiple of its file's own pixels, default 1 (1:1). An SVG, recognized by a data:image/svg+xml source, a .svg path, a blob: URL whose Blob type is image/svg+xml or a path without an extension that decodes at 300x150, as an SVG without its own size does, and whose HEAD response has Content-Type image/svg+xml, has no pixels, so its maxZoom is a multiple of the fitted size, default 4. A photo whose file is no wider than its fitted width, and a photo whose first load failed until a retry loads it, has no zoom: no zoom button, no zoom cursor and no double tap. With a srcset of width descriptors the widest candidate gives the pixels, since a zoom fetches it. Otherwise, until the file decodes, the item's width stands for its pixels; an item without one waits for the decode, whose width is the candidate's own and not the density-corrected naturalWidth
- tapZoom?: number, default 2.5, as a multiple of the fitted size; a double tap, a click and the zoom button zoom to the larger of tapZoom and 1:1, at most the maximum. A tall or a wide photo ignores it (see Shapes)
- preload?: number, neighbors kept loaded on each side, 0 to 2, default 1. The current image loads first with fetchpriority="high"; the neighbors start with fetchpriority="low" once it has decoded or failed, nearest first, and at each distance the one in the direction of the last change of photo before the other (forward: +1, -1, +2, -2; backward: -1, +1, -2, +2; open counts as forward), and not at all while navigator.connection.saveData is true
- controls?: Partial<Record<LightboxControl, boolean>>, where LightboxControl is "close" | "arrows" | "counter" | "zoom" | "fullscreen" | "download" | "slideshow"; the first four default to true, the last three to false. A control turned off is not in the DOM. Without close, focus starts on the viewport and Escape still closes
- thumbnails?: false | "bottom" | "side", default false. "bottom" is a strip under the caption that runs from edge to edge of the photo area and down to the bottom edge: a @reuse-sh/carousel with align center, mouse drag, native touch scrolling and mandatory snap to each thumbnail's center, padded so every thumbnail, the first and the last included, can sit in the center. A bottom thumbnail is --re-lightbox-thumbnail-size tall and as wide as its item's width and height give, at least 24px and at most three times its height, cropping a wider photo around its center with object-fit: cover, and square when the item lacks either. A ring stands still over the strip's center and takes the current thumbnail's size over 150ms, at once on open, after a resize and under reduced motion. A drag, a swipe or a wheel on the strip makes each photo current as its thumbnail's center comes closest to the ring, momentum included, and swaps it in place without paging; the photos show their previews while the strip moves, and the full files load once it has been still for 180ms or settles. The photo it stops on gets one change event and one announcement then, not one per photo passed, and the tab stop and any focus inside the strip move to its thumbnail. The scroll listener reads no layout: it reads the strip's scroll offset once per frame against thumbnail positions read once per layout. Any other change of photo moves the strip so that photo's thumbnail sits under the ring, at once on open and under reduced motion and smoothly otherwise. A click on a thumbnail opens its photo while the strip still moves, as a pagination button does; only a mouse drag that moved the strip skips its click. "side" is a column at the inline end edge (right in LTR, left in RTL) under the top bar, a native scroll container of square thumbnails with mandatory snap, the same ring at its middle and the same paging by a swipe or a wheel, though not by a mouse drag, and becomes the bottom strip while the window is narrower than 720px. The dialog carries data-thumbnails="bottom" or "side". The strip is a role="group" named strings.thumbnails and one Tab stop: only one thumbnail has tabindex 0, the active one again whenever focus leaves the strip, ArrowLeft and ArrowRight (swapped in RTL) or ArrowUp and ArrowDown in the column, Home and End page the viewer to that photo, the arrows wrapping past either end when loop is on, and focus follows its thumbnail as the strip brings it under the ring. Enter or Space opens the focused thumbnail's photo. The active thumbnail has aria-current="true". Below 480px of height the thumbnails are hidden, and they hide with the controls
- caption?: "bottom" | "side", default "bottom". "bottom" shows the caption centered under the photo, at most 60ch wide and scrolling inside past 20vh. "side" moves it into the aside panel, which then exists whenever some item has a caption, so the photo keeps one width while paging; an empty caption takes no space
- slideshow?: { interval?: number }, ms between photos, default 4000
- autoHide?: number | false, default 2500, ms of a still mouse before the controls hide; false keeps them up
- className?: string, added to the dialog
- icons?: Partial<Record<LightboxIcon, () => Node>>, where LightboxIcon is "close" | "previous" | "next" | "zoom" | "zoomOut" | "fullscreen" | "fullscreenExit" | "download" | "slideshow" | "slideshowPause"; a key without one keeps the built-in SVG icon. Zoom, fullscreen and slideshow show zoomOut, fullscreenExit and slideshowPause while zoomed, in fullscreen and playing; without that key they show the built-in second icon when their own key is not given either, else keep the given one. The previous arrow keeps the class re-lightbox-prev
- renderContent?: (context: LightboxContentContext) => undefined | (() => void), an overlay above the image; the returned function runs when the slide unloads. Media slides do not call it
- content?: readonly LightboxContent[], the modules for items with a type (see Media slides)
- autoplayMedia?: boolean, default true; a player starts the first time its slide is current after the open flight, when open() ran inside a user action (navigator.userActivation.isActive, assumed where missing)
- loop?: boolean, default false, no effect for one item. Pages through the carousel loop while the slides cover the viewport, and through the fade otherwise
- history?: boolean, default true at the top level and false in a frame, one history entry without a URL change; Back closes the lightbox with the usual flight, another close goes back only when the top entry is the lightbox's own, and opening again adds no entry. An entry that a close or destroy() leaves under another lightbox's entry is skipped with one more back once it becomes the current entry. While the lightbox has an entry, history.scrollRestoration is manual, and the page's own value returns one task after the entry is left, so the back after a close does not undo a scroll made during the flight
- strings?: Partial<LightboxStrings>
- slots?: Partial<Record<LightboxSlotName, LightboxSlot>>
- protect?: boolean, default false; turns off the photo's context menu, its long-press callout and the copy key (see The photo as a file)

createLightbox copies the options object, so changing that object later changes nothing. setOptions merges new values into the copy, and every option takes effect at the next open, except loop and strings, which also change an open viewer at once: loop as below, strings by relabeling the dialog, the strip's aria-roledescription, the controls, the thumbnails, those append() and setItems() build later included, the slides, the counter and a failed slide's message. The live region keeps its last announcement until the photo changes.

Large galleries. Every item has an empty slide in the strip for native scroll snap; an image and renderContent mount only within preload of the current photo and in slides on screen while the strip moves. The viewer reads the size of one slide and places slide k at k × that width, so a frame of paging costs the same with 1000 photos as with 10, and a change of photo rewrites the state of the two slides and thumbnails it concerns. Thumbnails load with loading="lazy", and only the active one is measured.

LightboxItem = LightboxImageItem | LightboxMediaItem. Both carry alt?, caption?, width?, height?, previewSrc?, thumbnailSrc? and origin? (HTMLElement | null | (() => HTMLElement | null), read again whenever the photo changes and after beforeclose, so a function can return a tile rendered later or a "+N" tile that stands for hidden photos). LightboxImageItem has no type, needs src: string, and takes srcset? and sizes?. LightboxMediaItem has type: string, which picks the module of that type in content, src? for the file, page or player link the module plays, and html? (() => Node); its previewSrc is the poster. An item with a type whose module is missing from content makes open() and setItems() throw a TypeError, and so does an image item without src.

Media slides. Each module is a separate entry that exports a factory returning a LightboxContent: { type: string; mount(container: HTMLElement, context: { item: LightboxMediaItem; index: number; signal: AbortSignal; autoplay: boolean }): LightboxMounted }, where signal aborts when the slide unmounts and autoplay is true when the player should start once current (the autoplayMedia option and an open by a user action), and LightboxMounted is { unmount(): void; pause?(): void; resume?(): void; aspect?: number, width over height, 16 / 9 when missing; interactive?: boolean, the slide takes pointer events; ready?: Promise<unknown>, settles once the player can show itself, while the poster and the spinner stay }:
- @reuse-sh/lightbox/video: video() for type "video", a video element with controls and playsinline from src with previewSrc as its poster.
- @reuse-sh/lightbox/audio: audio() for type "audio", an audio element with controls from src at the bottom of the slide over its poster. It loads video.js.
- @reuse-sh/lightbox/iframe: iframe({ sandbox? }) for type "iframe", the page at src.
- @reuse-sh/lightbox/embed: embed({ sandbox?, providers? }) for type "embed", where providers is a readonly list of EmbedProvider { name, pattern, url(match, item), aspect? } that this module alone tries, in order, before the built-in ones; nothing is registered globally. YouTube watch, youtu.be, live and embed links play from www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/<id>, Shorts at 9:16, and Vimeo links from player.vimeo.com/video/<id>, an unlisted video's hash as h; the link's query parameters other than v carry over, t (30, 30s or 1m30s) as start in seconds. A URL no provider matches opens about:blank. It loads iframe.js.
- @reuse-sh/lightbox/html: html() for type "html", the Node item.html() returns, in div.re-lightbox-html that scrolls. HTML strings are never parsed.
An iframe loads only an http: or https: URL; anything else, an empty URL included, becomes about:blank. Every iframe gets allow="autoplay; fullscreen; picture-in-picture", referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" and loading="lazy". The sandbox option sets the sandbox attribute. Without it, every frame of the iframe and embed modules, providers passed to embed() included, whose URL has another origin than the document gets sandbox="allow-scripts allow-same-origin allow-forms allow-presentation allow-popups", so it cannot navigate the host tab, and its popups keep the sandbox, so they cannot navigate it either; a same-origin URL loads unsandboxed. The frame of a media slide carries data-media=<type>, shows the poster (item.video.poster, else the tile's loaded image, else previewSrc) as img.re-lightbox-image, and holds the player in div.re-lightbox-media above it; it is 16:9 unless the item has width and height or the module an aspect, fitted like a photo with at least 24px at each side. While ready has not settled the frame carries data-loading with the 300ms spinner. A poster with a visible origin flies like a photo and the player mounts once the flight lands; a media slide without a poster fades and scales from 0.94 in place. Only the current slide plays: a change of slide calls pause() on the one left, or unmounts it when it has no pause (the iframe module and blank embeds), and resume() on the new one, which plays only the first time unless the person started it; the video and audio modules retry a play the browser refuses (NotAllowedError) muted and settle ready on loadedmetadata, and the embed module sends one muted play when the player has not started 1s after the first. YouTube and Vimeo get their commands through their postMessage APIs. Close pauses every player at once and the slides unmount when the dialog goes. An interactive slide's div.re-lightbox-media carries data-lightbox-interactive and data-carousel-no-drag, and touch-action: pan-y: pointers over it reach the player, the viewer's taps, double taps, zoom and dismiss swipes ignore it, and paging works from the 24px beside it, the arrows and the keys. Media slides have no zoom, no download and no copy key. lightboxItemFromElement reads data-lightbox-type on the trigger, its image or its link: the item then takes that type, the link's href, else data-lightbox-src, else the image's source as src, and the image's source as previewSrc, the poster. bindLightboxGallery follows a clicked trigger whose type has no module in the lightbox's content, and leaves such items out when another trigger of the group is clicked. A media slide is fitted whole at its aspect and never opens as a tall photo.

Trust. Items read from markup are untrusted: data-lightbox-type, a link's href and data-lightbox-src come from whoever wrote the page. The embed module plays only its built-in providers and those passed to it, every frame from another origin is sandboxed, and the download link offers only http:, https: and blob: files and data:image/png, jpeg, gif, webp and avif URLs. A same-origin URL is trusted and loads unsandboxed, so an open redirect on the host sends the frame to any page without a sandbox; the final origin of a redirect is unknowable before it loads.

Images. Each slide's full file loads in a detached img with the item's src, srcset and sizes; without sizes the viewer sets sizes to the photo's fitted width as "<ceil(width)>px" and updates it on resize, and the browser picks the candidate for the screen's density. 150ms after the zoom scale last changed, sizes becomes "<ceil(fitted width × scale)>px"; after a return to scale 1 it goes back to the fitted width, or the item's sizes, on the next idle callback. A file is decoded with decode() before it replaces the shown one, and a narrower file never replaces a wider one, so zooming out keeps the sharper pixels. The visible img.re-lightbox-image takes the decoded file's URL as its src and carries no srcset. Until then it shows the origin's loaded currentSrc when the origin is an img or contains one, or else previewSrc, so the open flight never carries an empty frame. While the open flight carries that preview, the full file waits for the flight to land, and while a pointer is down on the current photo it waits for the release, so the zoom under the finger keeps its bounds. The frame takes the item's width and height, else fits the screen at the preview's or the tile's proportions, since their pixels are the tile's and not the file's, and once the file decodes it takes the file's size around the same center, over 220ms on cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1), or the zoom duration when that is shorter (none under reduced motion) when that slide is current and the open flight has landed. An item without a width takes the widest w descriptor of its srcset, else the decoded file's own width, never the density-corrected naturalWidth. While a frame has no full file it carries data-loading, and theme.css paints it with --re-lightbox-placeholder. An SVG item without both width and height shows once its text is fetched (same-origin, CORS, data: or blob:) and parsed: the root's width and height in px, unitless, in, cm, mm, pt or pc give its size, else the viewBox's width and height, which also complete a single absolute width or height; a failed fetch or parse falls back to the decoded natural size. The frame carries data-transparent while the img it shows has a URL in a format that can carry alpha: a .png, .apng, .webp, .avif, .gif or .svg path in any case, query and hash ignored, a data:image/ URL of one of those types, a blob: URL whose Blob type is one of them, or a path without an extension that decodes at 300x150 and whose HEAD response's Content-Type is one of them, each looked up once per URL; a failed lookup counts as opaque. Any other path without an extension, such as a CDN URL with ?w=, sends no HEAD request and counts as opaque. JPEG and every other format count as opaque. base.css paints a frame with data-transparent and no data-loading or data-error with --re-lightbox-media-background, #f2f2f2 in theme.css, so the backing shows only under the photo. A first load still running after 300ms adds span.re-lightbox-loading role="status" with aria-label strings.loading and a 32px @reuse-sh/loaders canvas, fading in over 150ms; a later, larger file loads without it. A failed first load sets data-error and shows div.re-lightbox-error role="status" with strings.error in a p and a button.re-lightbox-retry with strings.retry, which loads the same src, srcset and sizes again; a failed larger file keeps the pixels on screen. Paging, zoom and close work as usual beside a failed slide.

The origin of the current photo is read again whenever the photo changes, to hide that tile with visibility: hidden, and once more after beforeclose, for the close flight and the focus. While the viewer is open only the current photo's tile is hidden; close and destroy restore it.

Close flies to the current photo's tile and lands there under whatever the page stacks above the tile, such as a sticky header: one copy of the photo flies in the dialog, opaque for the first 70% of the flight's time and fading out over the rest, so a container that clips the tile does not cut the photo before it nears the tile, and a second copy, an img.re-lightbox-landing inside an absolutely positioned box over the tile that clips it, placed right after the tile, or right after the carousel when the tile is a direct child of a .re-carousel viewport, flies on the same clock in the tile's own layer and lands opaque. The box keeps the copy out of every scroll container's scrollable overflow, so a strip at its last photo does not grow and snap during the flight. For those 300ms that parent has one more child, which nth-child rules and scripts that count children see; a carousel's slide count never does. Both are removed once the flight ends. A close during a settling move ends the move at once and flies from where the photo is on screen. A close during a held mouse drag or a touch fling ends it and disables the strip for the flight, puts the strip on the current photo at once and flies from where the photo was dragged, so no neighbor shows. The photo fades in place instead when the tile is detached, fails checkVisibility(), has no size, or lies outside the window or outside an ancestor that clips its overflow. The tile is read again after beforeclose and hidden for the flight like the first one. A close during the open flight reverses it from where the photo is, with no second copy. A close while zoomed or dragged unwinds both in the same flight. The close releases the scroll lock at once and holds the dialog at position: absolute at the current scroll position until it is removed, so a page that scrolls meanwhile carries the photo with the tile. The dialog takes the page's client size then, so a classic scrollbar that comes back adds no horizontal scroll, while the viewer inside keeps the size it had open, so the photo does not move on the first frame and the flight, measured from the live viewport, lands on the tile. For the flight the dialog leaves its modal state and stays above the page as an inert manual popover, so the wheel, touch scrolling and clicks reach the page; where popovers are missing it stays modal. Focus the visitor moves during the flight stays where they put it. Both flights take the tile's corners from its border radius, or, when it has none, from the nearest ancestor with the same box that clips its content, such as a rounded link around the img, each corner on its own.

Open focuses the close button, else the viewport; an open from a click or a tap does it without a ring, by the same means as below. After close, unless the visitor moved focus during the flight, focus goes to the current photo's tile, or to its closest link or button, when the tile is in the document and rendered, and otherwise to the element that had focus before open, always with preventScroll. A close by Escape or by a key on the close button shows the focus ring. A close by a mouse, a finger, a pen or Back does not: focus uses focusVisible: false, and where an engine still matches :focus-visible, as WebKit does after a keyboard open, the element carries data-re-focus-quiet until the next key or blur. theme.css hides the outline of [data-re-focus-quiet]:focus-visible; a page with its own focus styles hides its ring by the same attribute.

LightboxState: LightboxState<Item>, every field readonly, holds isOpen, isClosing, isAnimating, index, count, item, canPrev, canNext, isPlaying, zoom, chrome. item is the object at index that open() or setItems() received, not a copy. canPrev and canNext are true when previous and next can move, with loop on and more than one item both are. isPlaying is true from play() or the slideshow button until pause(), the button again, a single-item list or the end of the session, also while the slideshow waits. zoom is { scale, x, y } around the viewport center. isAnimating is true during a flight, a paging move or an animated zoom. chrome is "shown" | "hidden", also data-chrome on the dialog. The controls hide after autoHide ms of a still mouse, on a zoom, at the start of a pinch and on a tap; a key, focus moving into them, a 24px mouse move from where they hid, a tap and a return to scale 1 show them. While the scale is above 1 only the close and zoom buttons come back: the counter, the arrows, the toolbarStart slot, the other top-bar buttons and slots, the aside panel, the strip and every footer child take visibility: hidden, and focus on any other control in the dialog moves to the viewport when the zoom starts. When the scale crosses 1 either way, focus on a control in the dialog that is no longer rendered moves to the viewport too, so a page may show its own zoom control at one scale and the zoom button at the other. Nothing hides them while a control is hovered by a mouse or keyboard-focused, that is focused with :focus-visible; a control focused by a click or a tap hides with the rest.

LightboxStrings: dialog ("Image gallery", the dialog's and the viewport's accessible name), slide(index, count, caption) ("Image 3 of 12", with ", caption" appended when the status passes one; each slide's label passes none), counter(index, count) ("3 / 12"), close ("Close gallery"), previous ("Previous image"), next ("Next image"), zoomIn, zoomOut, fullscreenEnter, fullscreenExit, download, thumbnails ("Choose image"), thumbnail(index, count) ("Show image 3"), slideshowStart, slideshowStop, loading, error ("Image could not be loaded."), retry, carouselRole ("carousel", the strip's aria-roledescription). index is zero-based.

Layout. The dialog holds div.re-lightbox-stage, which fills it and holds every other part, so a theme selects parts as descendants of .re-lightbox. The top bar holds the counter and then the toolbarStart slot at the start, and the zoom, slideshow, fullscreen and download buttons, the toolbarEnd slot and the close button at the end. The arrows sit at the sides at mid height and are hidden below 480px of width. Previous sits at the inline start and next at the inline end, so on a right-to-left page previous is on the right, next on the left, and an icon node inside either is mirrored with scale: -1 1. The built-in previous and next SVGs carry the class re-lightbox-arrow-icon and are mirrored the same way outside those buttons, so a slot's icon("previous") points the right way. The footer holds the caption, the footer slot and the bottom strip. On each page the caption fades out and back in over --re-lightbox-fade-duration, 45% out with ease-in while it rises 0.4em and 55% in with ease-out as it rises from 0.4em below, downward instead when paging back, swaps its text while hidden and ends on its own opacity; the shift adds to the caption's own translate, and a page during the fade goes on from the opacity and the shift on screen, and an open, a list change or reduced motion sets it at once. Every inset includes the safe area.

Fullscreen. Browsers refuse fullscreen on a dialog element, so button.re-lightbox-fullscreen requests it on div.re-lightbox-stage and exits it when the stage is the fullscreen element. It is not rendered when document.fullscreenEnabled is false, as on an iPhone. Its label is strings.fullscreenEnter or strings.fullscreenExit, and the dialog carries data-fullscreen while the stage is fullscreen. Leaving fullscreen keeps the viewer open; Escape in fullscreen leaves fullscreen first, as the browser decides. Close and destroy leave fullscreen.

Download. a.re-lightbox-download with the download attribute links the widest width candidate of the current item's srcset, else its densest, else its src. It is hidden when that URL is not http:, https:, blob: or a data:image/ URL of type png, jpeg, gif, webp or avif. A blob: or data: file downloads natively. For an http: or https: file, same-origin included because its server can redirect to another origin, pointerdown and an Enter or Space keydown on the link start one fetch per URL, and focus alone fetches nothing. A plain click while that fetch runs or after it succeeded saves the blob under the file name of the response's Content-Disposition header when the page can read it (filename* in UTF-8 first, then filename), else under the decoded last segment of the path, the raw segment when it does not decode, and revokes the object URL 60s later. A click after the fetch failed follows the link with target="_blank" rel="noopener", and a fetch that fails after the click opens the URL with window.open and noopener. The tab the viewer is in never navigates.

Slideshow. button.re-lightbox-slideshow starts and stops it; its label is strings.slideshowStart or strings.slideshowStop and the dialog carries data-slideshow="playing" while it runs. It moves to the next photo every slideshow.interval ms, and from the last photo without loop goes back to the first the way goTo(0) does, so the photos between never show. It waits while the zoom scale is above 1, while the current slide's video or audio plays, until its pause or end, while the current slide is an embed, until the visitor pages, while a flight, a dismiss drag or its return runs, while a mouse is over the controls, while the document is hidden, and while a slot holds an element with aria-expanded="true", an open details or an open popover, and resumes with the time it had left; the end of the current slide's video or audio moves to the next photo at once, and any change of photo starts a full interval. --re-lightbox-slideshow-progress on the slideshow button runs from 0 to 1 over each interval, every frame, so only the button restyles; under reduced motion it stays 0 and photos change at once. One photo hides the button and stops the slideshow.

LightboxSlotName: "toolbarStart" | "toolbarEnd" | "footer" | "aside".

LightboxSlot: (context: { lightbox: Lightbox; document: Document; icon(name: LightboxIcon): Node; counter(): HTMLElement }) => { node: Node; update?: (state: LightboxState) => void; destroy?: () => void }. icon returns a new node for the icons option's key, else the built-in SVG, so a slot swaps icon("zoom") for icon("zoomOut") in update. counter returns a new span.re-lightbox-counter, aria-hidden like the built-in counter, that shows strings.counter for the current photo, rolls its changed digits the same way, at once under reduced motion, and hides with one photo, whether controls.counter is on or off. Call it when the slot mounts: the viewer keeps every counter it returned until the dialog closes. toolbarStart sits after the counter at the start of the top bar, toolbarEnd before the close button, footer between the caption and the bottom strip, aside in a side panel 320px wide that the photo fits beside, and above below 720px; the dialog carries data-aside while the panel exists. Slot elements take the theme's button style through the re-lightbox-button class, follow the markup in the tab order and hide with the controls. update runs on every published state change and once after mounting; destroy runs once when the dialog closes.

LightboxEventMap: { open, opened, change, settle, closed: LightboxEvent; beforeclose: LightboxCancelableEvent }. LightboxEventName is keyof LightboxEventMap.

LightboxEvent: { type, index, slide: HTMLElement | null }. LightboxCancelableEvent adds cancelable, defaultPrevented and preventDefault(), and only beforeclose carries it. open runs in open(), opened once the flight ends, change when the index changes or another item takes the current index, settle once after each move of the strip stops and not after the instant jump to the photo open() starts at, beforeclose synchronously before the close flight is measured, and closed once the dialog is removed, also when open() replaces an open viewer. close() and closed wait until the history entry is gone, so a navigation right after them stays. preventDefault in beforeclose keeps the dialog open.

Lightbox methods:
- open(items: readonly LightboxItem[], index?: number): void; an error thrown by a slot, renderContent or an origin function during open() removes the dialog, the scroll lock and the history entry, and open() rethrows it
- close(): Promise<void>, resolves once the dialog is removed
- next(), prev(), goTo(index: number). A goTo or a thumbnail click to a photo more than one away pages in like the next photo: the target arrives beside the photo on screen, from the side its thumbnail is on, with the usual paging motion, and the photos between never show. A second jump during the move keeps the arriving photo where it is and brings the new one beside it. A looping strip pages the same way. Reduced motion lands at once.
- play(), pause(): start and stop the slideshow of the open viewer, as its button does; play() does nothing while closed, while closing or with one item, and the slideshow control need not be shown
- zoomTo(scale: number, focus?: Point), scale clamped between the current photo's minimum (1, or the whole-height scale of a tall photo) and its maximum; toggleZoom(focus?: Point), from scale 1 to the tap zoom around focus, and from any other scale back to 1 around focus
- getState(): LightboxState, the last snapshot subscribers received, the same object; a drag, a pinch or a wheel shows up once it settles, and the photo's live position stays internal
- subscribe(listener: (state: LightboxState) => void, options?: { signal?: AbortSignal }): () => void, the state after each discrete action, completed pointer gesture, and wheel input after 150ms idle
- on(type: K, listener: (event: LightboxEventMap[K]) => void, options?: { signal?: AbortSignal }): () => void, for K extends LightboxEventName
Both stop when the returned function runs or the signal aborts, and an already aborted signal adds nothing. The same listener added twice is one listener. destroy() stops every listener and takes its abort listener off the caller's signal.
- append(items: readonly LightboxItem[]): void, adds images at the end without moving the current one; during the close flight it adds them to the list that waits for the close, so close(); append([c]); append([d]) on two images ends with four
- setItems(items: readonly LightboxItem[], index?: number): void, replaces the list; the current image stays when the list still holds it, matched by object and then by src, otherwise index or the nearest image left is shown, with a change event and the slide's announcement. An empty list closes the viewer with the usual close, beforeclose included, but that beforeclose has cancelable false and its preventDefault() does nothing, also when setItems runs inside a beforeclose listener. A list passed during the close flight waits until the dialog is removed, so the flight and the focus go to the photo on screen, and then sets count and clamps index; while closed, setItems sets them at once
- setOptions(options: Partial<Omit<LightboxOptions<Item>, "container">>): void, replaces the given keys, a nested object such as strings or controls as a whole; container is fixed at creation
- getSlide(index: number): HTMLElement | null
- destroy(): void, idempotent, restores the page scroll, the hidden thumbnail and every style and listener it touched

bindLightboxGallery<Item>(root: HTMLElement, lightbox: Lightbox<Item>, options?: { selector?: string; item?: (trigger: HTMLElement) => Item | null; group?: (trigger: HTMLElement) => string }): () => void. Opens the triggers inside root in lightbox, in page order, on a primary click without modifiers that no one prevented, and returns the function that removes its click listener; the lightbox stays the caller's to destroy. It throws a TypeError at bind when lightbox is not a lightbox from createLightbox, of this copy of the package or another, such as an options object of the old call shape. A click is left to the page, never cancelled, when the lightbox is destroyed or open() throws. Triggers are the elements that match selector, other than a lightbox's own and its landing copy; by default they are buttons, images and the links that hold an img or carry data-lightbox-type or data-lightbox-src, so a text link to a file such as "Download full size" stays a link, and a link with the download attribute, or an img inside one, is never a trigger; a match inside another match counts as the outer one, so a link around an image is one trigger. item(trigger), lightboxItemFromElement by default, gives each trigger's item, and a trigger it returns null for is left to the page, so its click goes on. Only triggers of the clicked trigger's group open, read from the closest data-lightbox-group or from group(trigger). When root has data-lightbox-options, bindLightboxGallery parses it as JSON once, at bind, and applies it only to a viewer opened from that gallery: each option in it fills an option the lightbox's own options (createLightbox and setOptions) leave unset, and each text string fills one the lightbox's own strings leave unset. The lightbox's own options return when that viewer closes, so galleries that share one lightbox each open with their own markup options, and a gallery without them opens with the lightbox's own. The JSON may hold imageClick, doubleTapZoom, maxZoom, tapZoom, preload, controls, thumbnails, caption, slideshow, autoHide, className, autoplayMedia, loop, history, protect and the text strings, such as close; text that is not JSON, a value that is not an object, any other key and a strings entry that is not text throw a TypeError that names root, such as "Invalid data-lightbox-options on div#prints.gallery: …".

lightboxItemFromElement(trigger: HTMLElement): LightboxItem | null. The image is the trigger when it is an img, else the first img inside it. Each data-lightbox-* attribute is read from the trigger, else from the image. src is the href of the closest a[href] when its path ends in .jpg, .jpeg, .png, .gif, .webp, .avif or .svg in any case, query and hash ignored, else data-lightbox-src, else the image's currentSrc; with none of them and no data-lightbox-type the result is null, so a link to a page is not an item. srcset and sizes come from data-lightbox-srcset and data-lightbox-sizes, previewSrc is the image's currentSrc, width and height come from data-lightbox-width and data-lightbox-height, else, only when src resolves to the image's own source, from the shown file's own width (its srcset candidate's w descriptor or density, not the density-corrected naturalWidth) at the image's natural proportions. caption is the text of the closest figure's figcaption, else data-lightbox-caption on the trigger, the image or its link. alt is the image's alt, else the trigger's aria-label or text, and the origin is the image, else the trigger, so a quick-view link without an image opens from the link.

CSS files: @reuse-sh/lightbox/base.css holds the layout and states (positions, sizes, control visibility, cursors, reduced motion) without colors or fonts in @layer re.base, and is required. @reuse-sh/lightbox/theme.css holds the default dark theme in @layer re.theme. base.css sets --re-carousel-gap and --re-carousel-slide-width on the viewport (0px and 100%) and on the bottom strip (the thumbnail gap and size), so @reuse-sh/carousel/styles.css on the same page leaves both layouts alone. base.css also applies the carousel loop's --re-carousel-shift as translate to the slides, so a looping viewer draws the photos across the seam without that stylesheet. --re-lightbox-control-hover defaults to --re-lightbox-control mixed with 12% of --re-lightbox-foreground, so a theme that sets only the control color gets a hover that matches it. Theme colors, radii, sizes, the font (--re-lightbox-font, system-ui and the platform sans-serif by default), font sizes (--re-lightbox-font-size 14px for the caption and the error, --re-lightbox-counter-font-size 13px), the controls' --re-lightbox-blur (16px), insets, paddings, the controls' transitions (--re-lightbox-control-duration 300ms, --re-lightbox-ring-duration 150ms), the scale a pressed control shrinks to (--re-lightbox-press-scale 0.96, while a mouse release anywhere over its resting size still presses it) and the timing (--re-lightbox-open-duration 400ms, --re-lightbox-close-duration 300ms, --re-lightbox-zoom-duration 350ms for a zoom by a button, a key, a click or a double tap, on --re-lightbox-ease (a pinch and a wheel follow the input at once), --re-lightbox-fade-duration 240ms for a fade to a distant photo and for the caption on each page, all read at open, 0ms making each instant, and --re-lightbox-ease; a flight whose easing the browser rejects uses the default curve) are --re-lightbox-* custom properties. --re-lightbox-padding, unset by default, is the space around the fitted photo, read at open and on each resize as the length it computes to inside the stage, so rem, calc() and clamp() work; without a length it is 32px. Under reduced motion both flights fade in place in at most 150ms. The controls take their opacity from --re-lightbox-chrome-opacity, which base.css sets to 0 while they are hidden and the dismiss swipe lowers; a theme that sets a control's own opacity multiplies it by that property. @reuse-sh/lightbox/themes/dark.css, light.css, page.css and photos.css, also in @layer re.theme, set only --re-lightbox-* custom properties and load after theme.css: dark repeats the default colors, light is a pale room, page takes --re-lightbox-page (Canvas by default) and --re-lightbox-page-foreground (CanvasText), and photos is white and turns black, with gray text, while the controls are hidden, over --re-lightbox-background-duration (300ms; 0ms in theme.css). dist/unlayered/ holds every file without layers.

The viewer is a native modal dialog paged by @reuse-sh/carousel. Escape closes it, also while zoomed. ArrowLeft and ArrowRight page, swapped on a right-to-left page, Home and End go to the first and last image, and + or = zooms in 1.5 times, - zooms out 1.5 times and 0 returns to 1. While zoomed the four arrows pan the photo by 10% of the viewport instead, the way a scrolled view moves; Ctrl+C or Cmd+C copies the photo (see The photo as a file); other keys with Alt, Ctrl or Meta are left to the browser. Keys act only while it is open. One image shows no arrows, no counter and no slideshow button. The visible counter is aria-hidden, carries dir="auto", so a counter of digits and marks reads left to right ("2 / 3") on a right-to-left page and one with right-to-left letters reads right to left, and rolls only the digits that change, each number kept whole; a role="status" region says strings.slide(index, count, caption) once the image changes, with the item's caption when it has one.

A touch or pen swipe whose first 10px run more vertically than sideways carries the photo, scales it to 1 - 0.25 × progress around the viewport center, so a tall photo's edge stays near the finger, where progress is the distance over half the viewport height, and fades the backdrop to 1 - progress and the controls to 1 - 2 × progress. When the swipe first passes 120px it calls navigator.vibrate(10) where the browser has it, once per swipe however often it crosses back. It closes past 120px or 0.5px/ms over the last 80ms before release, flying from where the photo is, and otherwise returns in --re-lightbox-open-duration. pointercancel never closes. A mouse drag never dismisses. A second finger during a dismiss swipe is ignored, so the photo stays with the first one. A close ends any gesture in progress, and a finger that lifts after it changes nothing.

The photo as a file. img.re-lightbox-image is a real img with the shown file as its src, so a right click and a long press give the browser's own menu with Save Image and Copy Image. It has draggable false and a dragstart over it is prevented, so a mouse drag on the photo pages at scale 1 and pans while zoomed, the same as a finger, instead of dropping the file elsewhere as macOS Photos and Google Photos do; saving the file stays on the Download control and the browser's context menu. Ctrl+C or Cmd+C, the key whose event.key is c, or, where the layout gives a non-Latin letter, the key at event.code KeyC, with no text selected and the focus outside a field writes the photo with navigator.clipboard.write as a ClipboardItem with an image/png Blob promise, and prevents the key. The shown img is drawn onto a canvas at its file's size, an SVG at the item's width and height; only when a cross-origin file taints the canvas is the file fetched again with mode cors and credentials same-origin, a PNG kept as fetched and another format drawn through createImageBitmap; where ClipboardItem or navigator.clipboard.write is missing, or ClipboardItem.supports("image/png") is false, the key is left to the browser. Copy works for same-origin, data:, blob: and CORS-served files; a cross-origin file without CORS headers is not copied, and that failure, like a refused write, is silent. protect: true prevents contextmenu on the photo, leaves the copy key to the browser and puts data-protect on the dialog, under which base.css sets -webkit-touch-callout: none and -webkit-user-drag: none on the photo.

Shapes. Scale 1 is the fit. A tall photo, more than twice as high as wide and more than twice the viewport height once fitted to the width, fits to the viewport width less the padding, never above its file size, and rests with its top edge at the top of the viewport; its minimum is the scale that shows it whole. At scale 1 and below a vertical touch or pen drag on it pans with the zoom's elastic pan and glide, except a downward drag that starts at its top edge, which dismisses; a horizontal swipe pages, ArrowUp and ArrowDown pan it by 10% of the viewport, ArrowLeft and ArrowRight page, and a vertical wheel pans it. Its double tap, click and zoom button go from 1 to the minimum and from any other scale back to 1. The open flight lands on the photo at rest, and a close flies from where it is. A wide photo, more than three times as wide as high, opens contained, and its double tap, click and zoom button zoom to the scale that makes it as high as the viewport, at most the maximum, around the tap.

Zoom. A pinch follows the fingers live from 0.6 times the minimum to 1.25 times the maximum around its first midpoint, and the photo follows the midpoint. When a finger lifts the other one pans on. Once the last lifts, a pinch below 0.75 times the minimum closes with the usual flight, below the minimum returns to it, and past the maximum returns to it around the fingers. A drag while zoomed pans, with the grabbing cursor on the viewport and everything in it, the element that holds the pointer capture: past an edge the photo follows 35% of the overshoot, and on an axis where it fits it follows 35% of the move and returns to the center. On release it glides to its position plus the speed over the last 80ms times 180ms, kept inside the edges, in 400ms on --re-lightbox-ease, and under reduced motion it stops inside the edges at once. While zoomed past 1 the strip does not page; after a drag comes to rest against the left or right edge (a tap, a pinch or a zoom does not count), the next horizontal touch or pen swipe toward that edge longer than 30% of the viewport width drops the zoom and pages, and a shorter one only stretches. A mouse drag at zoom only pans. A wheel without Ctrl or Meta does nothing at scale 1, except on a tall photo, and pans inside the edges while zoomed. Ctrl or Meta with the wheel, which is also how browsers report a trackpad pinch, zooms by exp(-deltaY × 0.01) around the pointer between the minimum and the maximum, and Safari's gesturestart, gesturechange and gestureend over the photo do the same while no finger is on the screen. The viewport fills the stage. The photo fits inside the viewport less the room the strip, the aside panel and --re-lightbox-bottom-room (unset by default, any CSS length, a percentage of the stage height) keep free at its edges, and above scale 1 it pans to the viewport's own edges, under them; a click or tap in the room the panel or the property keeps, beside the photo, never closes. A wide photo's double tap fills the viewport's height. The viewport's box is measured when the dialog opens, when a gesture starts and on resize, and no pointermove, wheel or gesturechange listener reads layout or computed styles. Resize events are handled once per animation frame of the dialog's window, which measures the box once. The current photo stays current, with no change event, and the strip stays on it. A photo at rest stays at rest in its new shape, so a tall photo keeps its top edge at the top. A zoomed or scrolled photo keeps the image point at the viewport center there: its scale is clamped to the new minimum and maximum, its pan grows with the fitted size, and it stays inside the edges. A resize ends a running flight or zoom animation at once: a flight at its last frame, a zoom where it is. A change of photo returns the photo to rest without animation.

Documents and frames. The dialog, the scroll lock, the focus return and the listeners belong to the container's document and its window, and each document counts its own open lightboxes. A lightbox in a frame locks only the frame's scroll, and two lightboxes on one page leave each other's scroll and focus alone. Element checks also accept nodes from another window, so a controller made in the parent page opens in a frame given a container there or, without one, when the photo's origin is in that frame, a gallery bound to a root in a frame opens in that frame, and a page that swaps in a new body gets the dialog in the new one.

@reuse-sh/lightbox/react exports useLightbox<Item>(options?: LightboxOptions<Item>): Lightbox<Item> and useLightboxState<Item, Selected>(lightbox: Lightbox<Item>, selector: (state: LightboxState<Item>) => Selected): Selected. useLightbox creates the lightbox on the first render and returns that object on every render; it holds no React state, so the component that calls it does not re-render when the viewer changes. After each render it passes the options that changed to setOptions, comparing plain objects and arrays by value and everything else, functions included, by identity, so an equal inline strings object changes nothing; container is read once. It never destroys the lightbox: effect cleanup, on unmount, in StrictMode's rehearsal and when Activity hides the component, closes the viewer at once without a flight and removes its dialog, listeners, timers, scroll lock and history entry, and the same object opens again when an effect runs again, so StrictMode and an Activity reveal keep one object and an effect such as useEffect(() => lightbox.open(items), [lightbox]) opens it again. destroy() stays the caller's and is final. useLightboxState reads the state with useSyncExternalStore: the component re-renders only when the selected value changes by Object.is, a selector that returns a new object re-renders on every state change and never loops, and the server and the hydrating render read the lightbox's state at creation, so a Suspense boundary that hydrates after open() matches the server's HTML.

@reuse-sh/lightbox/carousel exports connectCarousel(lightbox: Lightbox, carousel: Carousel, options?: { slideOf?: (index: number) => number }): () => void. On every settle and on beforeclose the page carousel goes to slideOf(index), the identity by default, with goTo(slide, "instant"), so the page behind is in place before the close flight is measured, even when the close comes right after paging. While the viewer is open it holds the carousel's autoplay with hold("lightbox", true) and releases it after closed; a pause() from the visitor stays. The carousel's own holds still apply, so focus returned to a tile inside the strip keeps autoplay waiting until it leaves. The returned function removes the link and the hold. The browser build ships it as browser/carousel-link.js with no imports, named apart from the carousel package's own browser/carousel.js.

Dialog attributes for styles: data-chrome, data-protect (present under protect), data-zoomed (scale above 1), data-zoom ("in" or "out", the way a click on the photo zooms next, as the zoom button's label says; base.css keys the zoom-in and zoom-out cursors on it), data-zoomable (true when imageClick is zoom and the current photo can zoom), data-panning, and data-phase, one of entering, opening, open, dragging, returning and closing. The React hook useLightbox(options) returns the controller itself, one object for the component's life, and passes changed options to setOptions; useLightboxState(lightbox, selector) re-renders only when the selected value changes. Both belong to the package and add no second implementation.
